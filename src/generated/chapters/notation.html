A Notation Reference and Symbol Table#

This section provides a unified reference for mathematical notation used throughout the paper. It is not exhaustive: symbols that appear only within a single section are defined where they are introduced and may not be listed here. Where a symbol is overloaded across subfields, the section-specific meaning is noted. All vectors are column vectors throughout the paper.

A.1 Dimensions and Indices#

  • d: residual-stream dimension; equivalently model hidden size

  • d_{\text{head}}: attention head dimension

  • d_{\text{mlp}}: MLP hidden dimension, typically d_{\text{mlp}} \gg d

  • N_H: number of attention heads per layer

  • d_{\text{sparse}}: dictionary size of an SAE or transcoder, d_{\text{sparse}} = e\,d with integer expansion factor e

  • L: number of layers (model depth); \ell \in \{1,\dots,L\} indexes layers

  • T: sequence length (context length); t \in \{1,\dots,T\} indexes token positions

  • O(\cdot): Big-O asymptotic complexity notation

A.2 Inputs and Activations#

  • x: an input prompt or a set of input prompts

  • h^{1:T}_\ell \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times T}: residual-stream activations at layer \ell for token positions 1 to T, whose t-th column is h^t_\ell

  • h_\ell^t \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: residual-stream activation at layer \ell for token position t

  • h_\ell \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: residual-stream activation at layer \ell with token position omitted

  • h \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: residual-stream activation with both layer and position omitted

  • h'_\ell \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: mid-block residual stream (post-attention, pre-MLP) at layer \ell

  • h_\ell^{\text{in}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d}, h_\ell^{\text{out}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: input and output of a model component, e.g., an MLP block, at layer \ell

  • \hat{h} \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: reconstruction of activations h by a dictionary model

  • \tilde{h} \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: activation after an intervention (steering, ablation, patching, etc.)

A.3 Dictionary Learning#

  • E : \mathbb{R}^{d} \to \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{sparse}}}: SAE encoder

  • D : \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{sparse}}} \to \mathbb{R}^{d}: SAE decoder

  • z \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{sparse}}}: sparse feature activations; z^i \in \mathbb{R} is the activation of feature i; when a layer index is also present, we write z_\ell^i for feature i at layer \ell

  • f_j \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: feature direction in activation space (the j-th column of W_{\text{out}})

  • W_{\text{in}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{sparse}} \times d}, b_{\text{in}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{sparse}}}: encoder weights and bias

  • W_{\text{out}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d_{\text{sparse}}}, b_{\text{out}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: decoder weights and bias

  • k: number of active features in a Top-k SAE

A.4 Model Parameters#

  • W_Q^r, W_K^r, W_V^r \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{head}} \times d}, W_O^r \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d_{\text{head}}}: query, key, value, and output projection matrices of the r-th attention head

  • W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{mlp}} \times d}, W_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d_{\text{mlp}}}: MLP weight matrices

  • b_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\mathrm{mlp}}}, b_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: MLP bias vectors

  • \theta: the collection of all trainable model parameters

A.5 Interventions and Objectives#

  • v_{\text{steer}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d}: steering vector, applied as h + \alpha\, v_{\text{steer}}

  • \alpha: scaling factor for an intervention

  • \mathcal{L}: loss function

  • \lambda: sparsity or regularisation coefficient. In Appendix C.2, \lambda(\cdot) instead denotes the local learning coefficient

A.6 Mathematical Operations#

  • p(y \mid x), \log p(y \mid x): conditional probability and log probability

  • \mathbb{E}[\cdot]: expectation over the data distribution; \mathbb{E}[\cdot \mid \text{condition}] denotes a conditional expectation

  • \|v\|: Euclidean norm; \|z\|_1 and \|z\|_0 denote the L_1 and L_0 norms

  • \langle v, w \rangle: inner product

  • \odot: element-wise product

  • \otimes: Kronecker product

  • \sigma(\cdot): activation function
